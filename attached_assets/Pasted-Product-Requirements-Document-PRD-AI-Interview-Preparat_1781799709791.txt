Product Requirements Document (PRD)

AI Interview Preparation Platform using MCP

Project Name

InterviewGPT – AI-Powered Interview Preparation Platform


---

1. Project Overview

Objective

Develop an AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps students and job seekers prepare for technical, HR, aptitude, and coding interviews through personalized learning, resume analysis, mock interviews, and performance tracking using MCP (Model Context Protocol).

The platform will act as a virtual interviewer capable of accessing multiple specialized MCP servers to provide accurate, personalized, and context-aware interview preparation.


---

2. Problem Statement

Candidates face several challenges:

Lack of personalized interview preparation

Difficulty identifying skill gaps

Limited access to mock interviews

No structured feedback mechanism

Poor resume optimization

Inadequate tracking of progress


The proposed system solves these challenges through AI-driven assessment and personalized recommendations.
